## Region 1: The Valley of Limits — Limits & Continuity

### Chapter 1.1: The Approach (Introduction to Limits)
- **Concept**: What is a limit? Approaching a value without necessarily reaching it
- **Visual Metaphor**: Lyra the Dancer approaches a point on stage — getting infinitely close but the spotlight defines the destination
- **Worked Examples**: lim(x→2) of (x²-4)/(x-2), lim(x→0) of sin(x)/x
- **Practice**: 10 problems (evaluate limits from graphs and expressions)
- **Creative Task**: "Choreograph Lyra's approach" — animate a point approaching a limit on a graph

### Chapter 1.2: One-Sided Limits
- **Concept**: Left-hand and right-hand limits; when they differ
- **Visual Metaphor**: Lyra approaches from stage left vs. stage right — different entrances, sometimes different destinations
- **Worked Examples**: Piecewise functions, |x|/x at x=0
- **Practice**: 10 problems
- **Creative Task**: Design a piecewise dance routine

### Chapter 1.3: Continuity
- **Concept**: What makes a function continuous? Three conditions
- **Visual Metaphor**: A continuous dance flows without breaks; a discontinuity is a stumble or teleportation
- **Worked Examples**: Testing continuity, types of discontinuities
- **Practice**: 10 problems
- **Creative Task**: "Repair the Dance" — fix discontinuous functions to make them continuous

### Chapter 1.4: The Squeeze Theorem
- **Concept**: Bounding a function between two others that share a limit
- **Visual Metaphor**: Two dancers converge on Lyra's position, squeezing her to a precise spot
- **Worked Examples**: lim(x→0) x²sin(1/x), lim(x→0) xcos(x)
- **Practice**: 8 problems
- **Creative Task**: Create a "squeeze choreography" animation

### Chapter 1.5: Limits at Infinity
- **Concept**: Behavior of functions as x → ±∞, horizontal asymptotes
- **Visual Metaphor**: Lyra dances toward the horizon — her height (y-value) settles to a level
- **Worked Examples**: Rational functions, exponential decay
- **Practice**: 10 problems

### 🎭 BOSS BATTLE: The Discontinuity Phantom
- Multi-phase fight testing all limit concepts
- Phase 1: Evaluate limits to dodge attacks
- Phase 2: Identify discontinuity types to find weak points
- Phase 3: Apply squeeze theorem to trap the phantom

## Region 2: The Derivative Conservatory — Derivatives

### Chapter 2.1: The Tangent Line (Introduction to Derivatives)
- **Concept**: Slope of tangent line, derivative as rate of change
- **Visual Metaphor**: Maestro Forte's baton traces tangent lines on a musical staff — the slope IS the pitch
- **Worked Examples**: Derivative from definition, slope at a point
- **Practice**: 10 problems
- **Creative Task**: "Conduct the Slope" — move a tangent line and hear pitch change

### Chapter 2.2: Basic Differentiation Rules
- **Concept**: Power rule, constant rule, sum/difference rule
- **Visual Metaphor**: Musical scales — each rule is a different scale pattern that Maestro teaches
- **Worked Examples**: d/dx(x³), d/dx(5x²-3x+7)
- **Practice**: 15 problems (drill-style)
- **Creative Task**: "Compose by Differentiating" — input a polynomial, hear its derivative as melody

### Chapter 2.3: Product & Quotient Rules
- **Concept**: Differentiating products and quotients of functions
- **Visual Metaphor**: A duet (product rule) vs. a call-and-response (quotient rule)
- **Worked Examples**: d/dx(x²·sin(x)), d/dx(x/(x+1))
- **Practice**: 12 problems

### Chapter 2.4: Chain Rule
- **Concept**: Derivatives of composite functions
- **Visual Metaphor**: Nested musical loops — a melody within a melody, each layer has its own rate of change
- **Worked Examples**: d/dx(sin(x²)), d/dx((3x+1)⁵)
- **Practice**: 15 problems
- **Creative Task**: "Nested Melodies" — compose a chain of transformations

### Chapter 2.5: Derivatives of Trig, Exp, Log
- **Concept**: Derivatives of transcendental functions
- **Visual Metaphor**: Each function family is an instrument section in the orchestra
- **Worked Examples**: d/dx(sin(x)), d/dx(eˣ), d/dx(ln(x))
- **Practice**: 15 problems

### Chapter 2.6: Applications of Derivatives
- **Concept**: Related rates, optimization, curve sketching, L'Hôpital's Rule
- **Visual Metaphor**: Using derivatives to compose the perfect symphony — finding the optimal tempo, the climax (max), and the quiet moment (min)
- **Worked Examples**: Optimization word problems, related rates, L'Hôpital's
- **Practice**: 12 problems
- **Creative Task**: "Optimize the Concert Hall" — find dimensions that maximize acoustic quality

### 🎵 BOSS BATTLE: The Tempo Tyrant
- Phase 1: Differentiate functions to match the Tyrant's tempo
- Phase 2: Apply chain rule under pressure (combo attacks)
- Phase 3: Optimize a function to find the Tyrant's weakness

## Region 3: The Integral Atelier — Integrals

### Chapter 3.1: The Antiderivative (Introduction to Integrals)
- **Concept**: Reversing differentiation, indefinite integrals, +C
- **Visual Metaphor**: Iris paints a picture and you must discover the original sketch — many sketches could lead to the same painting (+C)
- **Worked Examples**: ∫x² dx, ∫cos(x) dx
- **Practice**: 10 problems
- **Creative Task**: "Find the Sketch" — given a derivative painting, reconstruct the original

### Chapter 3.2: Definite Integrals & Area
- **Concept**: Definite integrals, area under a curve, Riemann sums
- **Visual Metaphor**: Filling a canvas with color — the area under the curve IS the paint on the canvas
- **Worked Examples**: ∫₀² x² dx, area between curves
- **Practice**: 12 problems
- **Creative Task**: "Paint the Area" — shade regions under curves to create artwork

### Chapter 3.3: Fundamental Theorem of Calculus
- **Concept**: FTC Part 1 and Part 2
- **Visual Metaphor**: The bridge between the Conservatory (derivatives) and the Atelier (integrals) — Iris reveals they were connected all along
- **Worked Examples**: d/dx ∫₀ˣ sin(t) dt, evaluating definite integrals via FTC
- **Practice**: 10 problems

### Chapter 3.4: Integration Techniques — Substitution
- **Concept**: u-substitution
- **Visual Metaphor**: Color mixing — substituting one pigment for another to simplify the palette
- **Worked Examples**: ∫2x·cos(x²) dx, ∫x/(x²+1) dx
- **Practice**: 15 problems

### Chapter 3.5: Integration Techniques — Parts & Partial Fractions
- **Concept**: Integration by parts, partial fraction decomposition
- **Visual Metaphor**: Collage technique — separating a complex piece into manageable strips
- **Worked Examples**: ∫x·eˣ dx, ∫1/(x²-1) dx
- **Practice**: 12 problems

### Chapter 3.6: Applications of Integrals
- **Concept**: Volumes of revolution, arc length, work, average value
- **Visual Metaphor**: Sculpting — rotating a 2D painting around an axis to create a 3D sculpture
- **Worked Examples**: Volume by disk/washer method, shell method
- **Practice**: 10 problems
- **Creative Task**: "Sculpt from a Curve" — rotate a function and see the 3D result

### 🎨 BOSS BATTLE: The Blank Canvas Colossus
- Phase 1: Evaluate integrals to fill in missing pieces of the Colossus's armor
- Phase 2: Use substitution to decode its attack patterns
- Phase 3: Calculate the exact volume to seal it in a crystalline sculpture

## Region 4: The Infinite Series Amphitheater — Sequences & Series

### Chapter 4.1: Sequences
- **Concept**: Definition, convergence, divergence, bounded/monotonic sequences
- **Visual Metaphor**: Verse recites lines of a poem — each line is a term; a converging sequence is a poem that reaches a conclusion
- **Worked Examples**: aₙ = 1/n, aₙ = (-1)ⁿ/n, geometric sequences
- **Practice**: 10 problems
- **Creative Task**: "Write the Next Verse" — predict and generate sequence terms

### Chapter 4.2: Infinite Series & Convergence
- **Concept**: Partial sums, geometric series, telescoping series
- **Visual Metaphor**: An infinite poem — does it converge to a finite meaning or diverge into chaos?
- **Worked Examples**: Σ(1/2)ⁿ, telescoping sums
- **Practice**: 12 problems

### Chapter 4.3: Convergence Tests
- **Concept**: Divergence test, integral test, comparison, limit comparison, ratio, root, alternating series
- **Visual Metaphor**: Each test is a different "poetic form" — a sonnet (ratio test), a haiku (comparison test), a limerick (root test)
- **Worked Examples**: Apply each test to classify series
- **Practice**: 20 problems (multiple tests per problem)
- **Creative Task**: "Match the Form" — classify series by choosing the right poetic/convergence test

### Chapter 4.4: Power Series
- **Concept**: Radius and interval of convergence, power series representations
- **Visual Metaphor**: A poem that can describe any story within its radius — the interval of convergence is how far the story reaches
- **Worked Examples**: Find radius of convergence, represent 1/(1-x) as power series
- **Practice**: 12 problems

### Chapter 4.5: Taylor & Maclaurin Series
- **Concept**: Taylor polynomials, Maclaurin series, error bounds
- **Visual Metaphor**: Approximating a masterwork poem with successive drafts — each term adds precision
- **Worked Examples**: Taylor series for eˣ, sin(x), cos(x), ln(1+x)
- **Practice**: 15 problems
- **Creative Task**: "Draft the Poem" — build Taylor polynomials term by term, watching them converge to the original function

### 📜 BOSS BATTLE: The Infinite Orator
- Phase 1: Determine convergence/divergence of the Orator's spells
- Phase 2: Find the radius of convergence to limit its power
- Phase 3: Construct a Taylor series to perfectly counter its infinite incantation
