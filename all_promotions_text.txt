 Drug Product Science & Technology 
Name: Jaquan Levons 
New Position:  Principal Scientist 
Department:  Engineering Technologies 
Supervisor:  Martin McLoughlin 
Location:  New Brunswick 105/2064A  
Degree/Institution:  BSE Chemical Engineering/Princeton University 
I am pleased to announce the promotion of Jaquan Levons to Principal Scientist. He expanded his people leadership, 
creative, strategy, culture and operational competencies to establish his readiness for his role of Digital Engineering 
Lead.  
Jaquan created Digital Engineering in DPST to harness the disruptive force of digital technology into new ways of 
developing drug products.  The Digital Engineering team is executing against a cohesive vision and strategy, which 
Jaquan developed and communicated, to achieve a holistic result for the organization. Jaquan influenced and motivated 
team members who contributed in a passionate, results-driven manner to ensure that the various pieces of the strategy 
supported the overall goal.   
Jaquan's Digital Engineering strategy enables projects with tools: 1) to push data in to scalable platforms with a focus on 
user experience 2) information accessible through various presentation technologies, and 3) next gen development and 
manufacture.  DE collaborators made advances in areas such as Lumetics data digitalization, voice to text for the lab, AR 
glasses for the lab, Sheldon telepresence technologies, 3D printing and scanning technologies. These technologies are 
maximally valuable as a collective in this strategy.   
The impact of these technologies are facilitating knowledge generation through scalable data and context capture, 
enabling rapid prototyping of machine parts, personalized medicine, and facilitated/enabled remote process transfer and 
monitoring. 
In creating the field of Digital Engineering in DPST, Jaquan defined the various roles of the digital engineer. He carved out 
time and space for passionate contributors across DPST to interrogate key technologies and their application in product 
development and manufacturing. In establishing such a role, with a defined career path, he established the relevance of 
the DE role within the DPST organization for interested scientists.     
Jaquan has broad influence, within and outside DPST. Jaquan’s views on digital engineering are consistently sought out 
by folks across BMS. Jaquan used his influence effectively to:  
 Initiate Alexa-in-the lab collaboration with Amazon,  
 Progress a POC for an information super highway,  
 Progress a POC development of augmented reality.   
As DE lead, Jaquan will be accountable for the Digital Engineering strategy for DPST, continue to lead his team, co-lead 
culture and talent development within ET, and help establish the strategic direction for systemic engineering projects. In 
addition, he will cultivate new “markets” for creative ideas in the digital space that enable, facilitate or accelerate 
development, manufacturing and supply chain activities. 
 
Please join me in congratulating Jaquan on his promotion and supporting his success. 
 
Martin Mcloughlin  
Head, Engineering Technologies & Device Development

more  than 18 years  industry experience spans application of first -principled based approaches to 
formulation develop ment  and investing in DPD people and culture.  Jaquan is an established formulator

of scientists across disciplines and modalities and has helped define and execute  the organization  
mission, strategy,  and culture  since ET’s inception .
