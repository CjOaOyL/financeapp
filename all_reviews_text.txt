Comment : DPD DevEx Modeling Lead
The SPD InSilico Formulation DevEx initiative was led through sprint cycles focused on data 
connectivity, model development, and dashboard deployment. A Model-First strategy was 
championed, enabling predictive modeling for viscosity, high molecular weight (HMW), and 
critical quality attributes (CQA). Integration across SPD, BD, PCO, and DBTx was facilitated, and 
modeling tools were embedded into dashboards to reduce empirical formulation work.
The Biopharm Digital program was launched with Biopharm Digital 0.1: ROVER in vitro, which 
included the development of the DPD PEMM ingestion tool and SPARTAN v2.3.0. Structured

followed by Biopharm Digital 0.5, which expanded the platform to include in vivo capabilities and

development were initiated to support future modeling and reduce reliance on animal studies.

secured across OPD, MSE, and DPD LT. Technical leads were supported in translating modeling 
outputs into actionable insights for scale-up and tech transfer. The DEM for RTD/CM initiative 
was similarly supported, with proven acceptable ranges (PARs) modeled to inform control 
strategies for clinical manufacturing. The FEM for tablet compression project was shaped 
through business case development and stakeholder alignment, enabling modeling to address 
tablet defects. The PIV project for SDD nozzle droplet modeling was positioned to support IND 
Tox and Phase 1 readiness through predictive modeling of spray drying dynamics.
The Spotlight Series strategy was designed and launched to showcase high-priority innovations 
in modeling, automation, and platforms. The DPD DevEx Platform Spotlight: SDD Platform & 
Tools session was curated and delivered, highlighting internalization of SDD development, 
platform compositions, and modeling integration.
